Domain Age Checker

Enter a domain to look up its creation date from public RDAP (Registration Data Access Protocol) records.

Limits, stated plainly: RDAP coverage varies by registry. Some registries block browser requests, some domains have privacy-protected records, and some TLDs have no public RDAP endpoint. If the lookup fails, the guide below explains how to check the date another way. A creation date is a fact from the registry; an age estimate from any other source is a guess.

If the lookup fails

Use whois.icann.org and search the domain, or use your domain registrar's WHOIS page. Look for the Creation Date field. Be aware that a domain's age is not a traffic signal on its own: a ten-year-old site with no content gets no traffic, while a one-year-old site with useful content can grow quickly.
